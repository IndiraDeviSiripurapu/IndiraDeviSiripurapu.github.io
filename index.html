01 / ABOUT

Research grounded
in everyday life.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of North Texas, working in the Smart Electronic Systems Laboratory with Dr. Saraju P. Mohanty. My doctoral research connects artificial intelligence, computer vision, and personalized dietary systems for smart healthcare.

My work moves from understanding what a person eats to modeling physiological response, recognizing dietary–medication risks, and designing recommendations that account for individual needs.

Before graduate research, I worked in enterprise Oracle database administration at Mphasis. Today, I bring that systems perspective to privacy-aware, explainable, and practical AI.
